# Planet textures

`textures/earth-atmosphere.jpg`, `textures/earth-normal.jpg` and
`textures/earth-clouds.png` are redistributed from the Three.js r160 examples:

- https://github.com/mrdoob/three.js/tree/r160/examples/textures/planets
- Upstream names: `earth_atmos_2048.jpg`, `earth_normal_2048.jpg`, `earth_clouds_1024.png`.
- Upstream MIT license: `three-textures-MIT.txt` in this directory.

All textures are served from this application. No external image requests are made at runtime.
All spacecraft, cockpit and obstacle meshes are generated by the application's own code.
